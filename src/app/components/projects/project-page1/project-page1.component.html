PROJECT CONSIDERATIONS

This project required a lot of coordination to make sure all of the technology enhanced the home without taking away from the architecture or interior design. One of the biggest goals was keeping everything as clean and seamless as possible, with minimal visible equipment and no technology distracting from the overall design.

We worked closely with the architect, builder, and interior designer to ensure all wiring, equipment locations, lighting controls, speakers, and user interfaces were integrated into the home properly from the beginning. The systems needed to be easy for the homeowner to use every day while also providing the flexibility and performance expected when entertaining guests.

Lighting was designed to create the right atmosphere throughout the day and evening, while the audio and video systems needed to deliver complete coverage throughout the home without impacting the aesthetics of the space. Careful planning and collaboration throughout the construction process ensured that both the technology and design objectives were achieved without compromise.

THE APPROACH

Technology planning began long before construction was complete, ensuring that infrastructure and system requirements could be thoughtfully integrated into the home's architectural framework.
Equipment, wiring pathways, and control interfaces were carefully considered to minimize visual impact while maintaining ease of use.
Every detail—from lighting control and motorized shading to speaker placement and entertainment systems—was evaluated in relation to the surrounding design, creating an experience that enhances the home without competing with it.
